About

I am an MBA in Finance candidate in the English-taught ICSI GMBA program at National Taipei University, Taiwan. Before coming to Taiwan, I studied Public Finance at the Academy of Finance in Hanoi, Vietnam.

My research focuses on how geopolitical risk affects financial markets and firms. I use empirical methods such as Local Projections and regression analysis. I am preparing to pursue a PhD in finance.
